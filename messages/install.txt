GunsNPrices
===========

Parse gun and ammo product titles into structured data: brand, model,
caliber, magazine capacity, barrel length, rounds per box and cost per round.

Open the Command Palette and type "GunsNPrices":

- Parse Titles: select one or more lines (or put the cursor on a line).
  One title opens a popup; several open the "gunsnprices" output panel.
- Parse Titles to JSON / to CSV: the same, as a new tab.
- Add Spec Columns to CSV: appends parsed_kind, parsed_brand, parsed_model,
  parsed_caliber and more columns to every row of a CSV or TSV file.
- Annotate Lines: shows the parsed specs at the end of each line.
  Clear Annotations removes them.
- Normalize Caliber: replaces the selected caliber text with its standard
  name, for example "9mm Luger" -> "9×19mm Parabellum".
- Cost per Round: price and round count -> price of one round.

Settings: Preferences > Package Settings > GunsNPrices > Settings.

Everything runs on your computer. The package makes no network requests.
The parser is open source (MIT, data CC BY 4.0) and is the one used by
https://gunsnprices.com to group listings from US gun and ammo stores.

Documentation: https://github.com/gunsnprices/sublime-gunsnprices
Other free tools: https://gunsnprices.com/developers
